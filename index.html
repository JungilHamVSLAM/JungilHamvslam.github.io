Jungil Ham

Ph.D. Candidate in Mechanical and Robotics Engineering

Gwangju, South Korea. 61005

I work on spatial AI for robots — robust visual localization and SLAM — from the International Space Station to aerial and field robots.

CV Google Scholar GitHub LinkedIn DBLP Email

I’m a Ph.D. candidate in the Machine Perception and Intelligence Lab (MPIL) at GIST, advised by Pyojin Kim. I build structure-aware visual localization: San Francisco World, a slope-aware visual compass (RA-L 2025); DTOR, a drift-free compass leveraging an ISS digital twin; and a monocular SLAM benchmark in microgravity on NASA’s Astrobee (ICRA 2026 Space Robotics Workshop).

Previously: research intern at the NASA Ames Intelligent Robotics Group; earlier internships at KIST and SKKU’s Robotics Innovatory. M.S., GIST · B.S., Kwangwoon University. Full details in my CV.
